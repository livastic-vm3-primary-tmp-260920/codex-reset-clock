FAQ

Common questions

1. When will Codex reset for me?

Open Codex or ChatGPT Settings → Usage and enter the exact reset time displayed there. This independent calculator does not log into your account or read your usage. Five-hour and weekly windows depend on plan eligibility.

2. Can this website force a Codex reset or show the next global reset?

No. It cannot reset your allowance, retrieve hidden account limits or predict discretionary global resets. Official resets are only confirmed by OpenAI or your account's updated Usage screen.

3. Do banked resets start a new weekly window?

A banked reset may refresh your eligible usage limits and change the weekly reset date when redeemed. Eligibility, expiration and covered allowances depend on the actual offer; see OpenAI's official reset guidance.

4. Why does the countdown disagree with my Usage screen?

This website uses the date you manually entered and your device clock. A purchased reset, redeemed banked reset, workspace policy change, clock or time-zone mismatch may alter the official deadline. Your account's Usage screen wins.

5. Do you collect my API key, account details or reset dates?

No account or API connection exists. The countdown runs in your browser. Dates are not saved unless you opt into local browser storage; the Clear button deletes that local record. GitHub Pages still sees ordinary network metadata needed to serve website files.

6. Is a paid reset the same as a saved reset?

No. A purchased instant reset applies immediately after payment for eligible accounts, while an available banked reset can be redeemed later subject to its offer terms. Official paid-reset rules ↗
